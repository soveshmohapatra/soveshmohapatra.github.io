My research examines how network structure shapes dynamics and function in the brain. I combine network control theory and machine learning for neuroimaging, and use large-scale text analysis to study scientific communication.

[Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=_IqJIZcAAAAJ&hl=en), [Semantic Scholar](https://www.semanticscholar.org/author/Sovesh-Mohapatra/2186185878), [ResearchGate](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Sovesh-Mohapatra-2)
